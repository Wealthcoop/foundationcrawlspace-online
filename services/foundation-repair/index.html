When does a Sacramento homeowner need foundation piers instead of cosmetic crack sealing?

Diagonal drywall cracks over doors, sticky window frames, sloping floors, and stair-step brick gaps wider than 1/4 inch point to soil settlement rather than shrinkage, requiring structural piers or soil stabilization.
